# Game localization

Produce a faithful, natural, usable localization of the owner's authorized game. Preserve the game's personality, speaker intent, relationships, mechanics and visual character. The agent owns judgment; tools protect mechanical invariants.

## Establish the job

Use the owner's exact source/build, language, scope and delivery request. Keep an immutable source and a separate private working candidate. Resolve only material ambiguity; do not ask again for established decisions. Respect content/safety exclusions and privacy. Do not acquire unauthorized copies, bypass DRM or redistribute copyrighted assets without permission. A translation request never authorizes external uploads, publication, extra spending or a paused task's resumption; preparation and status questions are not translation authority.

Before substantial work, identify all player-facing text surfaces—including active custom providers and all important image text—and state what is included, preserved or blocked. For content with age/participation boundaries, establish source-bound eligibility across those providers before translation, with independent read-only screening; unresolved eligibility stops translation and runtime assembly, and excluded content stays excluded. Adult-profile authorization is not a safety exception.

Establish coherent milestones within a finite project-wide effort/call budget, including review and delivery. Reassess at useful checkpoints, not every tiny batch. When scope or usage expands materially, stop at that checkpoint and ask for direction rather than extending the program silently.

## Translate coherently

Work by scenes, events or UI families, not arbitrary tiny field quotas. Use enough source and audiovisual context to preserve meaning, agency, voice and continuity; write natural English rather than literal word substitutions. Keep approved names, register and terminology consistent where the function is the same and allow contextual differences. Do not invent missing source meaning or silently repair the game's mechanics.

Preserve stable identities, executable syntax, controls on their owning records, placeholders, choice relationships and structural emptiness. Use the established local validator for those invariants and completeness. Add a targeted check only when the existing check misses a concrete risk. A complete standard catalog is not proof that custom providers or image text are covered.

Images remain unchanged by default. Bounded inventory/OCR, exact-asset or named-batch replacement, and runtime inclusion need their respective owner approvals; none implies the next. For authorized image work, preserve meaningful art and composition without cuts or obscuring panels. Inspect source lettering and the final composite at native and intended display scale; verify readable in-game fit and provide a legible before/after comparison before inclusion approval. Do not guess illegible lettering or expand permission to excluded assets. Preserve and disclose unresolved items, including important untranslated image text.

## Own the work, not the ceremony

Use one writer and one durable private working root. Delegate substantial, coherent work only when authorized and worthwhile. Give the worker the outcome, canonical inputs, exclusions, allowed output and budget—not a replay of the entire history. Use the short [batch worker reference](references/batch-localization-worker.md) for that brief. Preserve unique outputs before temporary worktrees disappear. Leave a compact handoff only when another executor actually needs it.

Keep translated coverage, independent acceptance and runtime proof distinct, using existing records rather than parallel status systems. Reuse unchanged source/target/context-bound evidence. Missing or stale labels are not defects; reconcile them before commissioning more work.

## Verify for the player

Use one independent, source-bound quality pass over the stable candidate or coherent shards. Check fidelity, naturalness, omitted surfaces and high-risk repeated families. State its coverage honestly; sampling is not whole-corpus review. Do not automatically stack batch review, another whole-corpus review and a third reviewer. Confirm repairs only where they change the evidence.

Completion requires structurally valid data and live in-engine proof. Run proportionate visible checks of important player paths, changed images and layout outliers. Check choices, controls and save/load where the deliverable depends on them. Expand testing for concrete defects or materially different consumers, not imagined completeness. Component proof is not natural-route or platform proof; disclose the difference.

When authorized to deliver, verify the actual package/readback and its usable scope, not merely the working files. Stop when the requested outcome and agreed evidence are satisfied. Report unresolved limitations without creating another campaign to tidy every ledger.

## Stop and communicate

Honor owner pauses immediately: halt translation, review and supervision; preserve work without optional integration or successors. A usage reset is not permission to resume. Follow the owner's quiet-update and delivery-channel preferences: no routine callback receipts or silence explanations; email only authorized BLOCKED/STOPPED/COMPLETED notices. Answer follow-ups concisely. Never call a partial or safety-excluded build complete.

Topical references and scripts remain available as technical help for a concrete problem, not automatic additional gates. Their workflow recipes are subordinate to this core, the current owner contract and higher-priority safety rules. Select only the needed material; do not load the library or inherit review chains. Acquisition and product-development procedures are not routine translation gates.
